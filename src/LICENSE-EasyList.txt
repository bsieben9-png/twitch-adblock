This package includes a trimmed subset of EasyList cosmetic (element-hide)
selectors only, used to hide leftover ad boxes on ordinary websites.
Network filter lines are not shipped in this file set.
Twitch, YouTube (family), and Kick are hard-excluded from this cosmetic path.
